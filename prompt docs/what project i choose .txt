Example

Manager:

"Why did customer complaints increase by 23% this month?"

Agent:

1. Query support database
2. Compare previous month
3. Group complaints by category
4. Check sales/product data
5. Search relevant internal documents
6. Find correlation
7. Generate evidence-backed explanation

Output:

Complaints increased primarily in Product X after its latest release.
68% of new complaints relate to login failures.
Support tickets increased 31% in this category compared with the previous month.

Then:

Suggested investigation: Check the authentication service logs for the release period.
